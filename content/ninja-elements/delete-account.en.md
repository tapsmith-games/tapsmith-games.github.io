# Delete your account — Ninja Elements: Idle RPG

Developer: **Tapsmith Games**

## In the game (fastest)

1. Open the game and tap your avatar (Profile).
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete account**, then tap it again to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

## Without the game

If you no longer have the game installed, email **{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}** with the subject "Delete account" and tell us:

- your in-game name;
- your player ID if you have it (shown under your name in Profile, can be copied);
- roughly when you last played.

We may ask a question to make sure the account is yours, and we delete it within 30 days.

## What is deleted

Your account, in-game name, avatar, progress (elements, fighters, items, currencies, leagues), quests and achievements, our records of your purchases, statistics and your clan chat messages.

## What may remain

- Copies in server backups for up to 35 days, after which they are overwritten.
- Automatic clan chat notices that mention your name (for example "Player joined the clan") until they drop out of the clan's latest 100 messages.
- Records kept by Google Play or the App Store about your payments, under their own policies.
