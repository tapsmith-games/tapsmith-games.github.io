# Privacy Policy — Ninja Elements: Idle RPG

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This policy explains what data the game **Ninja Elements: Idle RPG** ("the game") collects, why, who it is shared with and how you can delete it. The game is developed and published by **Tapsmith Games** ("we", "us"). Contact: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

## 1. What we collect

**You do not need to create an account or give us your real name.** When you first open the game, it creates a random installation ID on your device and uses it to sign you in.

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Random installation ID and a sign-in token (we store only a hash of the token) | To sign you in and keep your progress |
| In-game name, avatar, frame and hero appearance you choose | Shown to other players in rankings, arenas and clans |
| Game progress: elements, fighters, items, currencies, leagues, quests, achievements and in-game statistics | To run the game |
| Records of in-app purchases (product, store order number and purchase token) | To deliver what you bought and prevent fraud |
| Days you were active and game session times (start and last request) | To understand how players use the game and improve it |
| Version of the game app you use | To find and fix problems in specific versions |
| Country (two letters, e.g. US) — from the region in your device settings or, if that is missing, from your IP address | Statistics by country |
| Moderation records: an account block or a chat ban, its reason and end date | To protect players from rule violations |
| Clan chat messages you send | To show them to your clan |
| Reports you send about other players (the reason and, for a chat message, a copy of its text), reports other players send about you, and the players you hide | To moderate the chat and protect players; messages of players you hide are not shown to you |
| Email address — **only if you enter it**; it is not verified and not used for marketing | To contact you about your account if you ask us to |
| Which version of these terms you accepted and when, and your confirmation that you are 13 or older | To prove consent and ask again when the terms change |
| IP address — **not stored in our database**; used only in server memory | To protect the game from bots and abuse, and to determine your country (using a local table on our server; the address is not sent to any other service) |

Our hosting provider may keep technical request logs, which can include IP addresses, for a limited time for security and operation of the service.

Game reminders (for example "your offline rewards are full") are scheduled by the game on your device. They do not require a notification token, and you can turn them off in the game settings or in your device settings.

The following Google services collect data under their own privacy policies:

- **Google AdMob** (rewarded ads you choose to watch) — advertising ID, device information and approximate location derived from IP address, to show and measure ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before ads are personalised. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Google Play Billing** — handles payments for in-app purchases. We receive only the order number and purchase token to confirm the purchase; we never see your card or payment details. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We do not collect your precise location (only your country, see above), contacts, photos, files or microphone recordings.

## 2. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the game:

- Railway (hosting of our game server and database);
- Google (Google Play, AdMob) as described above.

Other players can see your in-game name, avatar, frame, hero appearance, rankings and clan chat messages. We may disclose data if required by law.

## 3. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we immediately delete your game data, the purchase records we keep, statistics, your clan chat messages, reports sent by you or about you, and your list of hidden players. Copies may remain in server backups for up to 35 days, after which they are overwritten. Automatic clan chat notices (such as "Player joined the clan") are kept only among the latest 100 messages of a clan and disappear as new messages arrive.

## 4. Deleting your account

- **In the game:** Profile → Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account and data immediately.
- **Without the game:** see {{DELETE_ACCOUNT_URL}} or write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

## 5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Legal bases (GDPR): performing our agreement with you (running the game), our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, improving the game) and your consent (personalised ads).

## 6. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in tokens are stored only as hashes. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

## 8. International transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.
